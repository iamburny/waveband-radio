## App name (30 max)

Waveband Radio: DAB+ for Cars

## Short description (80 max)

DAB and DAB+ radio for Android car head units, using a USB DAB tuner stick.

## Full description (4000 max)

Waveband Radio turns your Android car head unit into a DAB and DAB+ digital radio. Plug a USB DAB tuner stick into the head unit, run a scan, and every station in range is ready to play, with presets on screen and your steering-wheel buttons stepping between stations.

YOU NEED A USB DAB TUNER
Waveband Radio does not stream radio over the internet. It plays what a USB DAB tuner stick receives over the air, so you need a compatible stick plugged into the head unit. It currently supports the Raontech-based USB DAB+ sticks commonly sold for Android head units (USB ID 16C0:05DC). Other tuners are not supported yet.

BUILT FOR THE CAR
• Landscape layout designed for head-unit screens, with large buttons you can hit at a glance
• Steering-wheel next and previous buttons step through your stations
• Day, night and automatic display modes, and four accent colours
• Keeps playing in the background while you use navigation or other apps
• Picks up where you left off: reopens on your last station
• Received over the air, so it keeps playing with no mobile signal and uses no data

STATIONS AND PRESETS
• Full scan of the DAB band finds every station in range
• Station list by multiplex or A–Z, with Music, Talk and Favourites filters
• 12 presets, saved with a tap on the Now Playing screen

WHAT'S ON
• Now-playing text from the broadcaster, such as the song and artist
• Station slideshow images where the station sends them
• Station genre, bitrate and signal strength
• Traffic announcements on the current multiplex

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads, no accounts and no tracking. The app has no internet access and collects no data.

Waveband Radio is built on open-source DAB software, including the omri-usb DAB library, libfec and mpg123. Their licences are in the app under Settings, Open-source licences.

Please set up stations and presets while parked, and keep your eyes on the road.
